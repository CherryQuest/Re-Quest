🌱 Seed Vendor Menus

One-time admin utility. Log in to the main Re:Quest app as an admin first, then click the button. This copies the built-in menu into Firestore's menu_items collection, stamps every item and every stores/{id} doc with its stall codename, and skips the "Menu not yet available" placeholders.

Already seeded before the codename update? Use the Backfill button instead — it stamps codes onto your existing data without duplicating anything.
